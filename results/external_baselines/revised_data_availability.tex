\section*{Data and Code Availability}
The main-method code, exact manifests, source-file hashes, per-image metrics, calibration records, aggregate results, and figure sources are available in the public repository \url{https://github.com/wunan-snsy/A-Three-Seed-Study-with-an-Audited-Regional-Router}. The audited external-baseline artifacts are archived under \path{output/external_baselines/}. CAVER-R50D results are recorded in \path{output/external_baselines/caver_protocol/condition_summary.csv} and cover all 14 protocol conditions on NJU2K, NLPR, and SIP. DFM-Net clean/corruption training checkpoints, logs, and complete-split evaluations are recorded under \path{output/external_baselines/dfm_training/}; the corresponding summary is \path{evaluation_full_summary.csv}. The DFM-Net adapter and its compatibility assumptions are documented in \path{tooling/train_dfm_jsonl.py}.

Original NJU2K, NLPR, and SIP images are not redistributed. They must be obtained from the respective benchmark providers and placed at the relative paths recorded in the manifests. Full-precision checkpoints are included only where redistribution and storage permit; otherwise they can be regenerated from the recorded scripts, seeds, and configurations. HDFNet source code and license were audited, and its official Baidu checkpoint entry was recorded, but no checkpoint was available in the execution environment; therefore no HDFNet numerical result is claimed. All reported numbers are traceable to versioned CSV or JSON files in the release package.
